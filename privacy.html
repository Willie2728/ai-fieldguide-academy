Privacy notice.

This draft documents the MVP’s behavior and must be reviewed for the final business, vendors, jurisdiction, and deployment before launch.

What the MVP collects

Lead forms collect name, email, consent, source, and submission time. Learner progress and brand selections are stored in the browser. Willie receives the messages you submit, a privacy-preserving session identifier, and limited page context.

Where data goes

Lead records are stored on the server and may be forwarded to the configured email/CRM webhook. Willie messages are sent to the configured AI provider. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive personal information.
